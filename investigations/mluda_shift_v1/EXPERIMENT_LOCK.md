**Single intervention:** Keep full MLUDA's exact original six source/target augmentation passes and full CE + LMMD + source/target SCL + occupancy objective, then add one shifted labeled-source supervised branch with coefficient **0.5**. Construct the shifted source from post-official-ILDA full-cube source/target channel mean and population SD with the already evaluated historical SceneShift recipe: affine alpha **0.7**, epsilon **1e-5**, per-patch multiplicative Gaussian noise SD **0.04**, smoothed additive Gaussian SD **0.015** via 5×5 average pooling, clamp to [0,1]. Use a separate CUDA generator seeded `seed+99173`. The additional supervised logits are `model(shifted_source, original_target)[3]`, and the additional loss is `0.5 × CE(logits, source_label)`. This extra forward updates model parameters and BN according to normal train-mode execution. It is a composite method ablation, not a claim that SceneShift alone caused a gain.
